## Direction and its uncertainty

**Adopted direction (user, 19 October 2026):** the [information lattice](../research/information-lattice.md). Group-orbit surrogates give exact equalities I(Y; gX) = I(Y; T(X)). They are complemented by a within-class cross-record shuffle (XR), because independent shifts and independent phases keep lag-free dependence; IS and G0 are not univariate cells. Architecture-matched learners on each cell decompose decodable label information into univariate, lag-free dependence and relative timing, crossed with second-order versus higher-order. pyspi p90 supplies an invariance taxonomy and named explanations; statistic-edge GATs are one compared family. Gate 1, synthetic validity and power, is pre-specified in [`research/information_lattice/README.md`](../../research/information_lattice/README.md); results are recorded there. Open: boundary with the sibling SPI–SPI "dependence character" line; whether to rename the `spigraph` package; this repository versus a new one.

**Demoted:** the 16 October forecasting selector — whether interaction reliability and diversity predict cross-channel benefit on unseen sensor systems. Abdelmalak et al. (2025) already recommend Granger-based dataset analysis for the channel-independent versus channel-dependent choice. Won (October 2026) diagnoses attenuated covariate use in time-series foundation models, and Pinet et al. (2026) find anomaly benchmarks mostly univariate. Together with CoRA, ChaTSFM, Choi et al. (September 2026) and FFORMA, the selector fails the "well, obviously" test. Details are in the [agenda addendum](../research/agenda.md). A generic "stats + GAT", "correlation is not causation", "graphs sometimes hurt" or "gate channel mixing" paper would still not meet the user's standard.
