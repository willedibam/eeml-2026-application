## Direction and its uncertainty

**Lattice parked (19 October 2026).** The user judged the information lattice "very narrow" even when broadened, and asked for its problem to be motivated as important or replaced. Assessment: it is a diagnostic with no downstream decision that model validation does not already make. Its exactness holds for the ablation but not the learner-relative estimate; our own probe missed R4env at a scrambled lag. Its strongest use, auditing benchmarks for relational signal, has been made twice (Abdelmalak 2025; Pinet 2026). **In-context interaction inference (19 October):** judged "bland" by the user; not pursued.

**Current candidate, under review (19 October):** the [wired/wireless brief](../research/wired-wireless-brief.md) proposes pyspi-biased attention for *C. elegans* activity, sample efficiency and signalling-layer interpretation. Independent literature evaluation recommends narrowing before implementation; this is a reviewer recommendation, not a user decision or empirical verdict. The user will return the evaluation to the originating agent for refinement. No worm experiment has been run, and the brief's original novelty, ground-truth and data claims require revision.

- **Material missing precedents, verified at primary sources on 19 October 2026:** [Toyoshima et al. 2024, Fig. 10](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011848) fit anatomy-informed activity models, perform virtual optogenetics and compare with Randi responses (correlations .250–.486 on progressively smaller, better-sampled pair subsets). Their recordings include sensory stimulation; do not describe them as an exact spontaneous-only precedent. [Kinger et al., SBTG v2, 8 September 2026, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02852v2) infer interactions from sensory-stimulus calcium recordings and evaluate against Cook/Randi references and neuromodulatory networks. [NetFormer, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f88264fcc54775ee1706116e90fe351a-Abstract-Conference.html) already targets interpretable attention-derived neuronal connectivity. These are closer precedents than brain phenotype classifiers; their existence does not establish their biological claims as correct. No exhaustive novelty review has been completed.
- **Interpretation boundary:** atlas responses are total perturbation effects, not labels of direct transmission mechanisms; absent direct wiring does not imply extrasynaptic transmission. [Creamer et al. v4, 18 May 2026](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.09.22.614271v4) report 82% of perturbation-data reproducibility using connectome-constrained dynamics and indirect paths, not 82% of edges recovered. [Randi et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06683-4) distinguish detected, equivalence-supported absent and uncertain responses; their 53 candidate purely extrasynaptic-dependent pairs use unc-31 evidence, and extrasynaptic signalling can be fast. [Ripoll-Sánchez et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7615469/) infer potential peptide pathways from expression, receptor binding and spatial assumptions. Treat these as different, overlapping reference layers. Model head ablation establishes model dependence, not worm mechanism.
- **Data feasibility correction:** [Simeon et al. v4, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.12091v4) includes 577 Lin2023 worms averaging eight recorded neurons; ~250 labels is collection-wide coverage, not per-animal coverage. Its normalization uses whole traces. The live [neural card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/qsimeon/celegans_neural_data) describes moving-average smoothing, unlike the paper's no-additional-smoothing release description; inspect actual pinned files/preprocessing before interpreting lags. Both that card and the [connectome card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/qsimeon/celegans_connectome_data) currently show MIT; connectome access is gated. The paper's CC BY-NC-SA licence does not verify all dataset terms. Usable spontaneous cohorts, pair coverage, source permissions and preprocessing remain unaudited.
- **Protocol correction:** failure of every individual SPI to beat correlation does not prove a joint statistic prior useless; success does not establish attention's incremental value. Compare joint regularized SPI models and matched fixed-graph models, conditional dynamical baselines and NetFormer; distinguish atlas-supervised selection from a held-out perturbation test. Recompute priors inside every animal-count subset and count calibration data. Test common drive, missing neurons, heterogeneous calcium filters and overlapping pathway timescales in simulation. A possible narrower contribution is faithful, stable statistical routing that improves held-out perturbation prediction with limited observational data; neither benefit nor novelty is established. Keep S2 exploratory pending stronger pathway evidence and the user's decision on the SPI–SPI thesis boundary.

**Demoted:** the 16 October forecasting selector — whether interaction reliability and diversity predict cross-channel benefit on unseen sensor systems. Abdelmalak et al. (2025) already recommend Granger-based dataset analysis for the channel-independent versus channel-dependent choice. Won (October 2026) diagnoses attenuated covariate use in time-series foundation models, and Pinet et al. (2026) find anomaly benchmarks mostly univariate. Together with CoRA, ChaTSFM, Choi et al. (September 2026) and FFORMA, the selector fails the "well, obviously" test. Details are in the [agenda addendum](../research/agenda.md). A generic "stats + GAT", "correlation is not causation", "graphs sometimes hurt" or "gate channel mixing" paper would still not meet the user's standard.
